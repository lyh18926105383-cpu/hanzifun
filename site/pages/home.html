How does visual learning help me remember characters?

Pictures, familiar components, and a short story give you another way to recall a written shape. HanziFun pairs these memory cues with practice and useful examples. Visual mnemonics are labeled as learning aids, separately from historical character origins.

Explore a character →
Is HanziFun suitable for bilingual kids and beginners?

Families can start with our existing Chinese picture stories and small sets of characters. This preview also introduces English-led learning paths for beginners. Young children can explore together with a parent.

I'm traveling to China or doing business there. Where do I start?

Start with a situation you expect to meet. Try our restaurant sample for words like water and please, then explore the planned travel and business pathways.

Do you cover HSK vocabulary and resources for teachers?

HSK 2.0 level 1 has 150 vocabulary entries; HSK 3.0 level 1 has 300 vocabulary entries and 246 recognition characters. Higher levels, textbook collections and teacher decks remain planned.

How do the flashcards work?

Try to remember the meaning, flip the card, then mark it familiar or choose to see it again. Your practice choices and completed sets are saved on this device and appear in My Learning. Spaced repetition schedules future reviews on this device. Account sync remains planned.

Try the free sample →
How can I find a meaningful Chinese name?

Explore a name's characters, pronunciation, and meaning together. Our name preview offers curated examples in different styles and connects each character to a learning card. It does not translate or analyze your English name.
